AI Prompt Leak Guard

Available for Chrome and Edge. Firefox in review.

Check sensitive text locally before sending an AI prompt.

A local-first Manifest V3 browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox. It checks prompts on 18 AI chat services.

  • Warn, redact or block secrets, personal data and your own custom sensitive terms before a prompt is sent.
  • All detection runs on-device. Prompt text is not sent to AI Prompt Leak Guard or CandidLoom.
  • No telemetry by default, and free local protection needs no account or payment.
  • Plus, INR 99 per person per month. Plus adds local inspection of readable uploads: text files, Word, Excel and PowerPoint. PDFs, images, audio and video are not inspected. Plus renews automatically through your licence key.
