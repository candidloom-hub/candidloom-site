AI Prompt Leak Guard

Chrome Web Store listing coming soon

Check sensitive text locally before sending an AI prompt.

A local-first Manifest V3 browser extension for Chrome and Edge. It checks prompts on AI chat sites such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity and more.

  • Warn, redact or block secrets, personal data and your own custom sensitive terms before a prompt is sent.
  • All detection runs on-device. Prompt text is not sent to AI Prompt Leak Guard or CandidLoom.
  • No telemetry by default, and free local protection needs no account or payment.
  • Plus — INR 99 per person per month adds local inspection of readable selected uploads.
