Privacy

This note covers the CandidLoom company website at candidloom.com. Product privacy is covered separately, below.

This website

  • The site is static HTML and CSS. It sets no cookies.
  • It loads no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and no third-party fonts or content.
  • There are no forms. Nothing you do on these pages is collected by CandidLoom.
  • The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and request time, to operate and secure the service.

If you email us

If you write to loomcandid@gmail.com, we use your message and address only to reply. Please do not send real prompts, customer data, passwords, keys or payment details.

AI Prompt Leak Guard

The extension checks prompts on your device and does not send prompt text to CandidLoom or an analytics service. Read the full AI Prompt Leak Guard privacy policy.
