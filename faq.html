The product

What does AI Prompt Leak Guard do?

It checks what you are about to send to an AI chat, like ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. If it spots a password, an ID number such as Aadhaar or PAN, a card number or client details, it pauses and lets you remove them before sending.

Does it send my prompts to you?

No. Every check happens in your browser on your own computer. Your prompts, files and browsing never reach CandidLoom or anyone else.

Which browsers does it work in?

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Mozilla Firefox. Install it from the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons or Firefox Add-ons.

Does it catch everything?

No tool can promise that. It catches more than 50 kinds of private details and is a strong safety net, but please keep reading what you send.

How do I cancel?

Can I get a refund?
